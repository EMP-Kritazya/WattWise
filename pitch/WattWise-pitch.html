Problem statement

One busy 15 minutes can set the whole month’s bill.

Many businesses pay for electricity twice. Once for the energy they use, and again for their demand: the highest 15 minute average power anywhere in the month.

In a restaurant that moment is usually the Friday dinner rush, when the fryer, oven, dishwasher and air conditioning all pull hard at once. Nobody sees it happen. It shows up weeks later on the bill, and that one moment sets the demand charge for the entire month.

How we solve it

Sense, decide, act. Every minute.

1

Owner sets the rules

Each appliance is always on, flexible or deferrable, with limits. The owner picks a peak cap.

2

Agents bring context

Fetch.ai agents fetch weather, grid prices and nearby events, like a Giants game down the street.

3

One hub reports

Every appliance reports to one ESP32 style hub each minute. Commands go back the same way.

4

The engine plans

It forecasts the next blocks and moves flexible load: hold the dishwasher, trim the HVAC a little.

5

Claude handles the hard calls

When no move inside the rules is enough, Claude picks one of the engine’s options within the owner’s authority.

Why owners can trust it

The owner holds the keys.

Tested code computes every number

kW, dollars and forecasts come from the engine. The AI only quotes them and never invents one.

Rules are hard limits

Always on equipment is never touched, and refrigeration never goes below 50%, not even in an emergency.

Energy is moved, not cut

Anything held back is caught up the same day. The owner can undo any decision.

It works without the AI

Switch Claude and every agent off and the kitchen runs the same, with safe defaults.
